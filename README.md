#Portfolio Restructuring and Rebalancing Project#
This project focuses on managing and optimizing an investment portfolio in response to changing market conditions. It examines the impact of factors such as 
interest-rate fluctuations and political developments on different market segments. The project evaluates large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap allocations and uses financial 
analysis and data-driven modeling to identify suitable restructuring and rebalancing strategies. The objective is to improve portfolio performance while maintaining an
appropriate level of risk.
